# Code of conduct

This repository's issue tracker is a support channel, not a general forum.
Keep reports factual and on-topic. We will edit or close issues that:

- Contain abusive, harassing, or discriminatory language
- Are spam, off-topic, or promotional
- Disclose a security vulnerability (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead)

Disagreements about a bug, a feature, or how something behaves are fine and
expected — keep them focused on the issue, not the person. Repeated abuse may
result in being blocked from this repository.
